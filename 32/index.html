Making instagram.com faster: Part 3 — cache first ↗

To improve page load times, Instagram implemented a cache-first rendering strategy that immediately displays stale feed data stored in IndexedDB while fetching fresh content in the background. To prevent race conditions where user interactions like likes or comments are lost when the server response arrives, the team developed a staging mechanism inspired by Git rebasing. This system captures user actions during the fetch period and replays them onto the updated server state. This approach resulted in significant improvements to display-done times for both the feed and stories tray, aligning web performance more closely with native mobile experiences.

Implementing Dark Mode in iOS 13 ↗

Instagram implemented system-wide dark mode support for iOS 13 by building thin, backwards-compatible wrappers around Apple's UIKit APIs. This approach allowed the team to maintain compatibility with older Xcode versions and iOS 12 while leveraging native dynamic colors and images. To facilitate testing without requiring iOS 13 beta builds, the team developed a 'fake dark mode' feature that forced the app to render dark styles on older OS versions. This strategy enabled broad internal testing and a consistent user experience across different iOS versions.

The future of Monzo Business Accounts ↗

Monzo is expanding its product offering by launching business accounts, aiming to serve as a comprehensive financial control center for business owners. The development process emphasizes transparency through a public Trello-based product roadmap that allows users to track progress and vote on upcoming features. Key planned functionality includes automated tax management, direct integrations with accounting software, and streamlined invoicing and payroll tools.
