How we scaled metrics aggregation by moving durable state into storage ↗

Datadog evolved its metrics aggregation architecture across three generations to overcome state-management scaling bottlenecks caused by high data volumes, late arrivals, and duplicate deliveries. The initial design used static partitioning and local state, which made horizontal scaling brittle and restarts operationally disruptive. A second generation introduced dynamic partition assignment with incremental state snapshots in blob storage, significantly improving elasticity but still suffering from complex state recovery during incidents. The final design removed durable state from the streaming aggregation layer entirely, shifting responsibility for deduplication, partial aggregate merging, and late data reconciliation directly into the storage tier. This allowed streaming workers to resume instantly at the live edge and eliminated the need to provision fleets for worst-case historical state reconstruction.
