BGP Role model: tracking the adoption of RFC 9234 ↗

Route leaks in BGP cause Internet traffic misdirection when autonomous systems propagate routing announcements beyond their intended relationships. RFC 9234 addresses this by embedding BGP Roles directly into session negotiations and attaching an Only to Customer (OTC) transitive attribute to enforce valley-free routing. Cloudflare evaluated global RFC 9234 adoption through local BGP Monitoring Protocol feeds and public collector datasets, identifying 67 adopting networks led primarily by Internet exchange route servers. The investigation also revealed that major Tier-1 providers were stripping the OTC attribute, leading to direct outreach with networks like Arelion to fix attribute propagation and restore leak prevention capabilities.
