Building Reliable Production AI with Durable Workflows ↗

Salesforce addresses the challenge of scaling AI interactions from simple prompt-response prototypes to long-running batch workflows processing thousands of records without losing state during worker crashes or transient failures. To solve this, the Agentforce Grid team adopted Temporal to implement durable workflows that decouple execution history from individual worker processes and define fine-grained retry boundaries at the batch and row levels. This architecture ensures that progress is checkpointed after every successful record, allowing retries to resume precisely where work stopped without re-executing completed model calls. In internal load tests, migrating to Temporal-backed durable orchestration reduced completion failure rates from 90% to 0% while improving P95 completion times by approximately 60%.
