We’ve just announced a new round of funding ↗

Monzo has secured £113 million in a new funding round led by Y Combinator Continuity and Latitude to support its continued growth. The company reports reaching over two million users and is actively expanding its service offerings, including US market entry and a new marketplace model for financial products. These funds will be used to scale operations and further the development of business accounts and integrated financial services.

The future of Monzo Business Accounts ↗

Monzo is expanding its product offering by launching business accounts, aiming to serve as a comprehensive financial control center for business owners. The development process emphasizes transparency through a public Trello-based product roadmap that allows users to track progress and vote on upcoming features. Key planned functionality includes automated tax management, direct integrations with accounting software, and streamlined invoicing and payroll tools. The rollout is being managed through a phased waitlist approach to ensure system stability and gather user feedback.
