Why global workers are driving demand for stablecoin payouts ↗

A Stripe survey of over 2,300 independent workers across 20 countries reveals that 57% would accept stablecoin payouts to avoid high fees and slow settlement times of traditional cross-border financial rails. In emerging markets across Latin America, Africa, and the Middle East, workers seek stablecoins as a hedge against local currency volatility, a mechanism for digital financial access, and a faster remittance method. Key requirements from workers include simple wallet onboarding, educational resources, and the ability to earn yield on platform balances. Platforms can lower friction by offering integrated digital wallets, as demonstrated by a Stripe pilot where 90% of workers opted for an integrated wallet over self-custody.

Analyzing the evidence that helps businesses win “product not received” disputes ↗

Stripe analyzed evidence packets from one million "product not received" disputes over a 16-week period to determine which factors correlate with higher merchant win rates. The study revealed that submitting full delivery confirmation alongside GPS tracking maps and signatures increased win rates by 44 percentage points for physical goods, while digital activity and telemetry logs improved win rates by 10 percentage points for digital goods. Furthermore, issuing refunds directly through Stripe and timing evidence submission until after delivery confirmation yielded major win rate gains. Stripe applies these insights to its Smart Disputes product, which automatically aggregates carrier fulfillment history and uses AI to optimize evidence packets.
