Migrating Counter Service storage: Design choices and learnings ↗

Grab migrated its anti-fraud Counter Service handling billions of daily requests from a wide-column store to Aerospike to reduce infrastructure costs and improve latency. The team decoupled the storage layer from business logic using a concrete facade with enum dispatch and redesigned the data model by collapsing multiple time-bucket rows into a single map-based record per counter. This schema redesign reduced disk footprint by two-thirds, cut node costs by half, improved p99 read latency by 50%, and eliminated the previous multi-step read-modify-write pattern on the write path. Safe deployment was achieved through shadow reading and writing with zero downtime.
