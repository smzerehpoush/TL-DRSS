Patching the PostgreSQL JDBC Driver ↗

Zalando addressed a critical issue where PostgreSQL logical replication caused runaway Write-Ahead Log (WAL) growth, leading to disk exhaustion on databases with low-traffic tables. The problem occurred because replication slots could not advance until a write event occurred on the specific table being monitored, preventing the cleanup of older logs. By modifying the PostgreSQL JDBC driver to acknowledge KeepAlive messages with the current WAL position, the team enabled replication slots to advance even during periods of inactivity. This upstream fix eliminated the need for 'dummy write' workarounds and successfully stabilized WAL growth across hundreds of event-streaming applications.
