Certificate Transparency Monitoring is now generally available ↗

Cloudflare has officially released Certificate Transparency (CT) Monitoring to general availability after resolving high alert noise caused by routine automated TLS certificate renewals. Previously, the CT alerting service operated independently from the internal issuance flow, resulting in spammy alerts for routine internal renewals because identifier lookup arrived too late in the certificate lifecycle. By switching to the SHA-256 hash of SubjectPublicKeyInfo (SPKI) generated before issuance begins, the alerting service can deterministically cross-reference log entries with Cloudflare's order database and suppress internal notifications. This architecture change ensures customers only receive actionable alerts for certificates issued by third parties without Cloudflare's knowledge.
